# Bathroom Design Recommendations and Product Links

Prepared: September 16, 2026  
Scope: Interior-design recommendations from our review, organized by bathroom. These are proposed selections, not an approved purchase order or a construction specification.

## How to use this document

- **Preferred** identifies the current design recommendation.
- **Alternative** identifies a substitute, not an additional material to combine with the preferred selection.
- Product links lead to official vendor pages or manufacturer specification sheets. Where a vendor exposes only a collection page, the exact color, format and reference are stated.
- Confirm physical samples together, exact finish/SKU availability, dimensions, and application suitability before ordering. Prices and stock are deliberately omitted.
- Bathroom 1 received a design assessment but has not yet received a complete, product-specific selection schedule. Its unresolved items are explicitly identified below.

## Approved vendors

- Tiles: [ELM Surfaces](https://www.elmsurfaces.com/), [MSI Surfaces](https://www.msisurfaces.com/), and [Porcelanosa](https://store.porcelanosa.com/us/).
- Plumbing fixtures and bathroom/cabinet accessories: [Kohler](https://www.kohler.com/).
- Light fixtures: [Lightology](https://www.lightology.com/).

## Coordinated direction across the three bathrooms

Use warm neutral backgrounds, restrained tile layouts, related fixture shapes, and consistently warm light. The rooms do not need identical colors or metal finishes.

| Bathroom | Identity | Main coordinating elements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Warm stone and walnut | Richest, most material-focused room | Warm mineral surfaces, walnut, soft vertical lighting |
| 2 — Green tile and light wood | Main color statement | Seafoam field, richer green niche, warm neutral floor, brushed nickel |
| 3 — Warm white and black | Crispest, most architectural room | Warm pale field, textured black niche, white vanity, matte-black fixtures |

For new lighting, the proposed direction is 3000K and 90 CRI or better, with dimming. Renderings do not establish the actual color temperature or color accuracy of installed lights.

# Bathroom 3 — Warm Pale Tile, Black Niche and Matte-Black Fixtures

**Reference:** Image 3, `archsynth_image (29).png`.  
**Important correction:** The dark slim tile in the rendering is inside a niche. The earlier recommendation to remove it as a standalone wall stripe was withdrawn. Retain a deliberate dark niche accent.

## Design direction

Keep the white vanity, quiet white countertop, frameless mirror, frameless shower glass and matte-black fixture identity. Use warm chalk or very light greige field tile rather than yellow cream. Simplify the geometric shower floor and surrounding decorative tile so the textured niche becomes the main accent.

## Preferred tile selections

| Location | Product and link | Format / finish / reference | Design recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shower wall field | [ELM Grounded — Alabaster](https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product/grounded-alabaster) | 12 × 24 in.; matte porcelain | Plain field, horizontal straight stack; no extra contrasting strip |
| Main bathroom floor | [ELM Grounded — Alabaster](https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product/grounded-alabaster) | 12 × 24 in.; matte | Continue the pale color family |
| Shower floor | [ELM Grounded — Alabaster](https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product/grounded-alabaster) | Coordinating 2 × 2 in. mosaic | Replace the geometric patterned floor; confirm the exact mosaic's shower-floor suitability |
| Niche back — preferred accent | [ELM Vivid — Ligne Noir](https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product-collection/vivid) | Nominal 3 × 10 in.; matte ceramic relief; catalog reference 204221E | Vertical stack with aligned joints; dark charcoal grout; texture provides the emphasis |
| Niche returns | [ELM Grounded — Alabaster](https://www.elmsurfaces.com/product/grounded-alabaster) | Same pale field material | Frame the recess through the change in material, without adding a separate decorative border |
| Niche sill / shelves | Smooth coordinating pale material; exact piece not selected | Not the relief tile | Provide a smooth surface for bottles |

Grounded is a terracotta/clay-look collection; Alabaster was selected for its pale character, not an orange terracotta effect. Confirm the undertone against the white vanity and sanitaryware.

ELM lists Vivid for walls and wet areas. The **Ligne relief is matte**; it is distinct from ordinary Noir. The linked [ELM Vivid catalog](https://assets-global.website-files.com/6508c8490fee3dfe75080d5e/6508c8490fee3dfe75083b55_ELM%20SURFACES%20-%20VIVID.pdf) identifies Ligne Noir as 204221E and gives nominal 3 × 10 in. sizing (actual 2.75 × 9.66 in.). Follow the manufacturer's joint guidance when setting out the niche.

# Final Sample and Selection Checklist

1. Review Bathroom 2's Seafoam, Caraibi, floor tile and nickel finish together under the proposed light.
2. Review Bathroom 3's Alabaster, Ligne Noir, white countertop and black finish together.
3. Measure both niche backs before finalizing vertical layouts and edge cuts. Bathroom 2's niche dimensions and appearance are not shown in the supplied image.
4. Keep niche relief on vertical backs; use smooth sill and shelf surfaces.
5. Confirm exact tile use, finish, order codes and current availability with the vendors.
6. Confirm basin/faucet reach, faucet mounting, trim/valve compatibility, cabinet pull centers and light/mirror proportions.
7. Complete Bathroom 1's product selections separately; its current section records the design direction only.
